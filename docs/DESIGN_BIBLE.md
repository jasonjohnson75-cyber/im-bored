# I'm Bored Design Bible

## Brand promise

**I'm Bored. Let's fix that.**

The product should feel like a polished consumer discovery app, not a city calendar, tourism brochure, family-only guide, nightlife directory, or coupon site.

## Audience rule

Design for broad appeal. Adults, families, couples, students, seniors, locals, and visitors should all feel equally welcome.

## Visual blend

Combine three qualities:

1. **Cinematic confidence**
   - strong imagery
   - bold headlines
   - immersive featured moments

2. **Bright social energy**
   - warm, optimistic accent color
   - friendly language
   - clear interactive states

3. **Premium local restraint**
   - generous spacing
   - neutral base
   - controlled color palette
   - strong typography

## Core palette

- Warm neutral background: `#F7F3EC`
- Surface: `#FFFDF9`
- Primary ink: `#1D2A23`
- Muted text: `#6F756F`
- Warm action accent: `#EF6A45`
- Warm soft: `#FEE6DC`
- Secondary green: `#2F6B52`
- Green soft: `#E1EBE4`
- Divider: `#E5DDD2`

Use dark cinematic surfaces selectively for featured content, not as the entire app background.

## Typography

Prefer modern sans-serif typography with high legibility. Headings should feel confident and compact. Body copy should remain calm and readable.

Rules:
- large headline with tight letter spacing
- no decorative script fonts
- minimum practical mobile body size around 15-16px
- avoid long centered paragraphs
- use sentence case for most labels

## Shape and spacing

- Cards: 20-28px radius
- Pills/chips: full radius
- Primary buttons: 16-18px radius
- Minimum tap target: 44px
- Mobile page gutters: 18-22px
- Strong vertical rhythm, avoid crowded dashboards

## Imagery

Photography should show the experience, not generic stock poses. Use a balanced mix of music, food, arts, sports, outdoors, family activities, community events, and local places.

Do not visually imply that the app exists mainly for nightlife, young adults, children, or tourists.

## Home screen hierarchy

1. Brand: **I'm Bored.**
2. Supporting line: **Let's fix that.**
3. Location + radius
4. Hero discovery card
5. Primary CTA: **FIND SOMETHING**
6. NOW / TONIGHT / WEEKEND
7. Mood/category chips
8. Two teaser recommendations
9. Bottom navigation

## Recommendation labels

Use these as meaningful editorial signals:
- Best Bet
- Free Pick
- Family Pick
- Something Different
- Worth the Drive

## Navigation

Primary mobile navigation:
- Home
- Explore
- Saved
- Me

Keep navigation persistent and thumb-friendly.

## Motion

Use restrained microinteractions only:
- subtle card lift
- button press feedback
- chip state transitions
- save animation
- smooth route transitions

Avoid constant background animation or visual noise.

## Accessibility

- WCAG-conscious contrast
- visible keyboard focus
- semantic buttons/links
- meaningful alt text when real imagery is added
- no color-only state communication
- support reduced-motion preferences

## Visual quality gate

Before production data is connected, score every core screen 1-10 on:
- hierarchy
- typography
- spacing
- imagery
- mobile usability
- navigation
- consistency
- accessibility
- perceived quality
- desire to use

Target: **95/100 total or better**.
